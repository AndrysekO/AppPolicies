# Photo Recipe Saver Support

Photo Recipe Saver keeps all your recipe photos and screenshots in one place, organized by meal, with ingredient scanning and a built-in shopping list.

## Common questions

**My recipes aren't showing up on my other device.**
Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID on both devices and that iCloud is turned on for the app in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes.

**The app can't access my camera or photos.**
Go to Settings > Photo Recipe Saver and allow access to the Camera and Photos.

**How do I delete my data?**
You can delete recipes inside the app. To remove data synced to iCloud, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
